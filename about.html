Who we are

Rooted in science, growing for the future

Founded in 1987, the Botanical Garden is an independent research and conservation organisation based in Namur, Belgium. Our teams study plant diversity, maintain living collections and share knowledge with the public.

Our work combines botanical research, environmental education, collection management and the technical infrastructure required to support a modern research institution.
